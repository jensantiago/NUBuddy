How it works

From “it's broken” to “it's fixed” in four steps

Every report follows the same clear path, so you always know where things stand.

Submitted

File your report with the building, room and a photo.

Under Review

Facilities sees your report and confirms the issue.

In Progress

A technician is assigned and working on it.

Resolved

The fix is done and you get notified.
